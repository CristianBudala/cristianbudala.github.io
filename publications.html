  1. 2026
    Sunflower-Free Uniform Families: Recursive Constructions and Explicit Bounds.
    E. Axante, C. Budala, D. Chitic, B. C. Dumitru, and M. Nacu. arXiv

    We introduce a recursive construction for sunflower-free families and use it to obtain a general lower bound on the exponential growth rate of f(w,k). We also prove a general upper bound for 3-uniform families with at least four petals. Our results give 39≤f(3,4)≤49, f(3,5)≤146, 153≤f(3,6)≤255, 259≤f(3,7)≤474, and 54≤f(4,3)≤83. In addition, we prove that the maximum size of an intersecting 4-uniform family containing no sunflower with three petals is 27.

  3. 2026
    Local Patterns and the Cost of Costas Array Search.
    B. C. Dumitru and C. Budala. HAL

    We pruned higher-order Costas-array searches using seven-dot patterns from smaller orders, recovering known classes through order 31, and projected a ~8 GPU-year cost for order 30.
